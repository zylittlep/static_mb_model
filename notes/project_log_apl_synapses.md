Built 2026-10-07 from public, no-login downloads (Google Cloud buckets of each project).

## Second version: `with_outside_ROI_synapses/` (2026-10-14)
The same tables, plots and maps (including `odor_weighted/`), with the synapses that lie outside every official MB ROI but belong
to the mushroom body added, in all four datasets and in the calyx, peduncle and lobes. Each added row is flagged in
`mb_region_source`. Rule in short: anchors are the ALPN→KC synapses of the official calyx and the APL↔KC synapses of the official
peduncle and lobes; both grow through outside synapses with KCs in ≤3 µm steps, and an outside synapse joins the region of the
nearest anchor point within 3 µm. Accessory calyces stay out. The rule, counts and a map of the added synapses are in that folder's
`README.md`, `added_synapses_summary.csv` and `where_synapses_were_added.png`. The files in this folder are unchanged.

## Key columns
- `direction`: `APL_output (APL presynaptic)` or `APL_input (APL postsynaptic)`.
- `pre_*_nm`, `post_*_nm`: presynaptic (T-bar) and postsynaptic site, **in nanometres** in each dataset's native space. Divide by 8 for hemibrain/male CNS voxels; FlyWire neuroglancer uses (4,4,40) nm; BANC (4,4,45) nm. FlyWire also has `ctr_*` (cleft centre).
- `partner_side_*_nm`: the partner's own site (used for claw/bouton assignment).
- `partner_id`, `partner_type`, `partner_class`: counterpart neuron. Blank type = untyped fragment/neuron (hemibrain 9%, male CNS 10%, BANC 39%).
  - Hemibrain (updated 2026-10-10): `partner_type` is the official neuPrint v1.2 type (read from the release's synapse-points file). `partner_type_harmonized` keeps the FlyWire-harmonised name from the BANC team's compiled hemibrain table (e.g. glomerulus renames such as VC3m_lvPN → VC5_lvPN). The compiled table had labelled ~200 α′β′ KCs as KCg-m, which is why the official type is now primary. `partner_class` still comes from the compiled table.
- `roi_official`: dataset's own ROI. Hemibrain / male CNS: ROI of the postsynaptic point (CA, PED, aL, a'L, bL, b'L, gL). FlyWire / BANC: MB_CA, MB_PED, MB_VL (α+α'), MB_ML (β+β'+γ).
- `mb_compartment` = finest label available, with `mb_compartment_source`:
  - Male CNS: official `malecns-subcompartments-v3` ROI volume (α1–3, α'1–3, β1–2, β'1–2, γ1–5).
  - Others: **derived** (no public compartment ROIs). Method: soft k-nearest-neighbour vote (k=15) over dopaminergic neuron (PAM/PPL1) presynapses inside the same lobe ROI; each DAN votes with its type's compartment distribution measured in male CNS. `compartment_vote_share` = confidence.
  - Validation on male CNS: 96.9% agreement with official ROIs when lobes are known (hemibrain situation); 88% with FlyWire/BANC's coarser VL/ML lobes, where **α'2 is essentially never recovered** (called α2) and γ3/β'1 are weaker. BANC has few DAN synapses in the lobes, so treat its compartments as rough.
- Claws / boutons (all **derived**, no published per-synapse claw/bouton annotations are publicly downloadable for these datasets):
  - PN boutons: single-linkage clustering (2 µm) of each antennal-lobe PN's T-bars in the calyx.
  - KC claws: single-linkage clustering (2 µm) of each KC's postsynaptic sites from PNs in the calyx. Gives 5.6–8 claws per KC (literature ~5–7).
  - An APL synapse gets `claw_id` / `bouton_id` if the partner-side site is ≤2 µm from that KC's claw / PN's bouton points. `nearest_claw_id` / `nearest_bouton_id` + `*_dist_nm` are always given, so you can re-threshold.
  - Result: ~30–35% of APL↔KC calyx synapses fall within 2 µm of a claw; ~98% of APL↔PN calyx synapses map to a bouton.
  - These are the old claws. The new skeleton-based claws (2026-11-04) are described in the third-version section above.

## Caveats
- The tables in this folder use each dataset's official ROIs only. The male CNS v1.0 CA(L)/CA(R) ROIs leave out the posterior part of each calyx (primary ROI `CentralBrain-unspecified`), so the male CNS calyx numbers here miss 18% (left) / 9% (right) of PN↔APL and 33% / 14% of KC↔APL calyx synapses, and 12% of ALPN→KC synapses. In FlyWire, hemibrain and BANC, 3–6% of APL↔KC and 0–5% of APL↔PN synapses next to the calyx fall outside every MB ROI (ventral edge, partly accessory calyx). `odor_weighted/calyx_shape_by_dataset.png` shows where. `with_outside_ROI_synapses/` has a second version with these synapses added (male CNS calyx +31% APL synapses; FlyWire and BANC lobes +25% and +57%, where CRE, SIP and SMP take in parts of the lobes).
- BANC APL synapse counts are much lower than other datasets (detector/proofreading differences); BANC labels part of the lobes as CRE, which is excluded here (added back in `with_outside_ROI_synapses/`).
- `summary_counts_by_compartment_and_partner_class.csv` was made from an earlier hemibrain table: 5 hemibrain γ3–γ5 rows differ from `hemibrain_APL_MB_synapses.csv.gz` by 1–4 synapses (found 2026-10-14, left as is).
- FlyWire `apl_side` comes from the FlyWire classification table (fly's side).
- Hemibrain/male CNS counts depend on release confidence thresholds (`conf_pre`, `conf_post` kept).
