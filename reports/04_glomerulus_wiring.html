Report 05 lets APL's inhibition differ between glomeruli in three of its versions (P, L and M). This report explains where those per-glomerulus numbers come from. Only hemibrain and male CNS are used (Yang, 2026-10-21), with official ROIs only. In the male CNS the two calyces are pooled here, because the per-glomerulus numbers are computed per dataset.

1The units: PN boutons, claws and microglomeruli

In the calyx, each PN axon forms boutons: swellings that carry its output synapses. Several KC dendritic claws wrap around one bouton, and the bouton with its claws is called a microglomerulus. APL makes synapses in both directions with both partners: APL→PN and PN→APL on the boutons, and APL→KC and KC→APL on the claws (Figure 1). Report 02 explains how boutons and claws are found from synapse positions (clusters of sites within 2 µm). A KC↔APL synapse counts as on a claw if its KC-side site is within 2 µm of one of that KC's claw PN synapses, the same rule that defines claws. Every claw belongs to one glomerulus: that of its main PN, the PN that makes most of its PN synapses.

2Six per-glomerulus numbers

Raw synapse counts mostly reflect how big a glomerulus is: how many PNs it has and how many claws. To compare glomeruli, each count is divided by a measure of the glomerulus's input to the KCs. For glomerulus ggg:

Two more divide the claw counts by the number of claws instead (cgoutc^{\mathrm{out}}_gcgout​ and cginc^{\mathrm{in}}_gcgin​, APL→KC and KC→APL synapses per claw). The model uses only pgp_gpg​ (version P, and M) and qgq_gqg​ (version L). pgp_gpg​ measures how much APL inhibition a glomerulus's boutons receive for each synapse they pass on to KCs. qgq_gqg​ measures how much APL inhibition its claws receive for each PN synapse they get.

Caveats

Synapse counts stand in for synaptic strength. Nothing here measures how strong a single APL→PN or APL→KC synapse is.

Male CNS official calyx ROIs miss part of the posterior calyx, so its calyx counts are lower than hemibrain's (Report 02). The ratios are less affected, because numerator and denominator lose synapses together.

Glomeruli missing from the response tables still have weights, but they carry no odor input in the model.
